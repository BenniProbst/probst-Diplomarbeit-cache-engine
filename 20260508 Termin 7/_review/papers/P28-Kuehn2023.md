## 1. Konzeptionelle Identitaet

**Daten-basierte Cache-Optimierung** von B+-Trees mittels **Histogramme + Hot Paths**. Probabilistic distribution wird genutzt, um haeufige Pfade in **kontinuierliche, cache-freundliche Memory-Locations** zu legen.

> "Considering the probabilistic distribution of lookup values to the B+-tree as part of a memory-friendly and cache-aware layout is a consequent next step. [...] Favoring frequently visited nodes and paths in the regard of cache hits can improve the overall performance of the tree" (P28:S.1/41-44)

**Klasse:** Probabilistic Layout-Optimierung (Build-Zeit / static)
**Performance:** Bis zu **26%** Improvement vs Default-Baseline

## 2. Hauptkonzept

### 2.1 Histogramm pro Knoten
- Counter pro Branch in jedem Knoten
- Gibt **relative Probabilities** der Subtrees
- Aus Counter werden **Hot Paths** extrahiert

### 2.2 Hot Path Layout
- **Hot Paths** werden in **linear ascending Memory** gelegt
- Hardware-Prefetcher laedt naechsten Hot-Path-Knoten **bevor er gebraucht wird**
- LRU-Eviction schuetzt Hot Paths automatisch (haeufig zugegriffen)

### 2.3 EXPLIZIT OUT-OF-SCOPE (PRT-ART-relevant!)

> "The question of a proper runtime collection of the empirical probabilities in the tree nodes and the **re-layouting of the tree upon changes in the probabilities during runtime is out of the scope of this paper**." (P28:S.2/152-155)

**→ DAS ist genau die PRT-ART-Forschungsluecke!**
